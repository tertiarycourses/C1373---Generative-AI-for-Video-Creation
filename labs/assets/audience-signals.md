These are invented classroom observations, not market research or real TikTok analytics.

- A busy office worker says: “I change several things at once, so I never know why the next cup tastes different.”
- A beginner says: “Ratios sound technical. I need a starting point I can see.”
- A home brewer says: “I often let the last drips run while I answer a message.”
- A team administrator says: “The pantry has basic equipment and no weighing scale at every station.”
- A learner says: “I save short checklists when the steps are visible on screen.”

## Content Constraints

- The hero video must teach exactly three checks: grind consistency, a 1:16 starting ratio, and contact time.
- The video should run for 25–35 seconds.
- The intended learning signal is a save-worthy checklist, not a purchase promise.
- Platform behaviour must be described as a hypothesis unless supported by current official guidance or supplied data.

## Source Boundary

Use these notes only as synthetic audience context. Do not state that they represent actual customers, prevalence, or verified market demand.
